# Procurement Policy (SOP-PROC-001)

**Owner:** Head of Procurement · **Applies to:** all purchases of goods and services · **Version:** 3.2

## 1. Purpose
This policy defines how the company buys goods and services through the Procure-to-Pay (P2P)
process so that spend is controlled, auditable and placed with approved suppliers at the best
total cost.

## 2. The Procure-to-Pay flow
1. **Purchase requisition (PR)** – an internal request raised by a planner, the inventory
   system (reorder trigger) or the OpsPilot agent. A requisition does not commit spend.
2. **Purchase order (PO)** – the legally binding order sent to the supplier. Every PO must
   reference a requisition, an approved supplier, a ship-to warehouse and agreed unit prices.
3. **Goods receipt (GRN)** – the warehouse confirms what physically arrived
   (see SOP-WH-004 Goods Receipt Procedure).
4. **Supplier invoice** – Accounts Payable matches the invoice against the PO and the GRN
   (3-way match, see SOP-AP-002) before payment is released.

"No PO, no pay": invoices without a valid PO are returned to the supplier.

## 3. Supplier selection
- POs may only be issued to suppliers with status **APPROVED** in the supplier master.
  Suppliers with status PENDING (onboarding not finished) or BLOCKED must not receive POs,
  even if their quote is cheaper.
- For repeat catalogue items, buyers request live quotes from the Supplier Portal and select
  the **cheapest approved supplier**, unless the lead time would miss the need-by date. In that
  case the fastest approved supplier may be chosen and the reason recorded on the PO.
- Above **$25,000** per PO, at least three competitive quotes are required.
- Above **$100,000**, a formal RFQ with documented evaluation (price 50%, quality 30%,
  delivery 20%) is required.

## 4. Maverick and emergency purchases
Purchases made outside the process ("maverick spend") are reported monthly. Emergency purchases
(line-down situations) may be ordered by phone but the PO must be created within one business
day and approved under the normal approval limits (SOP-FIN-001).

## 5. Changes and cancellations
Price or quantity changes to an approved PO require a PO revision that is re-approved if the
new total crosses an approval threshold. POs can be cancelled only while no goods have been
received.

## 6. Records
All POs, quotes and approvals are retained for 7 years.
