## Cleaning rules applied

- **Dates**: ISO, `MM/DD/YYYY`, `DD.MM.YYYY`, `YYYYMMDD` and `Mon DD, YYYY` normalised to ISO dates; unparseable required dates reject the row.
- **Units of measure**: free text (`each`, `pcs`, `kgs`, `carton`, `litre` ...) mapped to `EA/BOX/KG/L/M`; unknown units (`??`, `bundle`) reject the item.
- **Numbers**: `$1,234.50`, `1,200`, ` 35 `, `12.0` parsed as decimals; negative quantities rejected.
- **Codes**: `p-1001`/`P1001` -> `ITM-1001`, `po 10001` -> `PO-10001`, `Whse B` -> `WH-B`.
- **De-duplication**: suppliers by normalised tax id, else normalised name; customers by normalised name + e-mail. Earliest record survives, blanks back-filled, legacy codes kept on `suppliers.legacy_codes`, and POs that referenced a duplicate vendor number are re-pointed to the survivor.
- **Referential integrity**: rows pointing to unknown items, suppliers, customers or warehouses are rejected (orphans) instead of loaded with broken FKs.
